## Recommended next incremental batch

The strongest next batch is **a small, versioned official-source knowledge catalogue**, not sensitive-data features. It can deliver three bounded items: a curated set of high-demand topic records, reusable “source/reviewed-on/limits” cards, and topic landing pages that connect each record to existing checklists and FAQs. This advances groups #5–7, #15–17, #24, and #25 while keeping the app independent, educational, and non-determinative.

The next batch after that should be a **structured low-data guidance interview** that asks only controlled, non-sensitive preparation questions and returns official source links or escalation routes. It should not calculate tax, accept documents, store financial details, or decide eligibility.

> **Deliberately deferred:** any CNIC/NTN, bank, document, tax-amount, asset/liability, notice-text, filing, submission, or personalised planning capability. These are not ordinary feature gaps; they require a separate privacy, security, retention, consent, and legal review before development.
