## Archive rules

The archive will label each entry as **Current Tax Year 2026** or **Historical Tax Year 2025**; show the source publication date; retain the original FBR source link; and state that only a later official FBR notice can confirm a deadline extension. It will not reproduce third-party commentary or infer a Tax Year 2026 extension from Tax Year 2025 history.

For the current filing-date reference, the archive will direct users to [FBR’s income-tax due-dates page](https://www.fbr.gov.pk/categ/income-tax-due-dates/51147/40846/81148), which lists 30 September for individuals and AOPs, 31 December for companies, and 30 September for companies with a special tax year.
