# Investment education sources

## Scope

This section is educational only. It does not rank products, give personalised suitability advice, estimate returns, assess a user’s tax position, or encourage a purchase. It directs users to authorised providers and official sources for current product terms.

## Reviewed sources

| Topic | Source | Content used in the section |
|---|---|---|
| ETFs | [Pakistan Stock Exchange — Exchange Traded Funds](https://www.psx.com.pk/psx/product-and-services/products/exchange-traded-funds-etfs) | Explains that an ETF can provide exposure to equity or fixed-income securities and aims to track a specified benchmark index while its fund price is available during the trading day. |
| Government securities / bonds | [State Bank of Pakistan — InvestPak](https://investpak.sbp.org.pk/what_is_government_bond/pages) | Official public education reference for Pakistan government securities. The in-app wording stays neutral and tells users to review the current official terms and risks. |
| Fixed-income savings products | [National Savings Pakistan — Products](https://savings.gov.pk/products/) | Lists official National Savings certificate and account categories. The in-app wording will not quote changing rates, maturity terms, or product eligibility. |

The implementation will not present current rates, maturity terms, or product comparisons because terms and rates can change.
