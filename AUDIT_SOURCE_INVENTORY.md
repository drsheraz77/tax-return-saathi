## Verified Page Scope

The FBR **File Income Tax Return** page identifies IRIS as the online portal where an income-tax return is filed. It also links users to registration, completing a return, revision, late filing, record keeping, privacy, withholding-tax, due-date, refund, and appeal guidance.

The FBR **Acts/Ordinance/Rules** index provides official destinations for the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001, Income Tax Rules, 2002, Finance Acts, and other legislation. A product link to this page should be described as a route to verify legal material, not as a substitute for professional advice or a claim about a user’s individual position.
