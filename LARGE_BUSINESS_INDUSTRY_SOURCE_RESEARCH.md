# Large Business / Industry Pathway — Source Research

**Research date:** 26 August 2026  
**Purpose:** Build a bounded education and preparation pathway for large businesses and industrial operators. This record is not a tax opinion, a legal database, or a basis for tax treatment, eligibility, timing, return acceptance, or FBR outcome claims.

## Guardrails

The pathway may introduce record readiness, official-registration review, return-workflow orientation, internal role coordination, and escalation to FBR or qualified advisers. It must not supply rates, thresholds, input/output tax calculations, eligibility, exemptions, sector-specific treatment, deadline calculations, return completion, notice interpretation, audit support, or claims about an industrial entity’s legal obligations.

## Verification observations — 26 August 2026

The FBR income-tax registration page explicitly identifies company and AOP e-enrolment in Iris, and presents Iris as the online income-tax system. The filing page confirms that filing occurs by logging into Iris and lists navigation topics such as return completion, revision, late filing, record keeping, and privacy. These observations support a process-orientation card only; they do not support a conclusion that a particular company should register, file, revise, or use a particular route.

The FBR sales-tax registration and filing pages provide official starting points for registered-person workflows and portal access. The industry path will link to these pages only as official destinations. It will not state that an industrial business must be registered, file a particular type of return, apply a rate, calculate a liability, or follow a particular deadline.

## Implemented mapping — 26 August 2026

The public Resource Hub now has a distinct **Large business & industry: tax-preparation starting points** category, separate from the renamed individual **Freelancer: registration & return preparation** category. The new industry category uses the five reviewed FBR destinations for registration, income-tax filing and record preparation, sales-tax registration, and sales-tax filing. The existing verified FBR income-tax filing destination is intentionally reused for the record-readiness card; this keeps the public link set bounded and avoids presenting the path as a legal retention requirement.

The knowledge catalogue and learning-path model likewise now distinguish an individual freelancer pathway from a company or industrial-operation pathway. The complex-situation navigator separates individual freelancer/remote-work questions from company/industrial-operation questions. Every new card and path retains the same no-determination, no-deadline, no-calculation, no-outcome, and qualified-help escalation boundary.
