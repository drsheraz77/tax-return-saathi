# Audit Feasibility Classification

## Short answer

No remaining audit group is **technically impossible**. However, several requested depths are deliberately **not implementable in the current product without changing its approved privacy, governance, and independent education boundaries**. They should be treated as blocked until their prerequisites are completed, rather than as ordinary feature backlog items.

The latest safe releases already expanded education, source transparency, low-data guidance, navigation, accessibility, discovery, and the strictly limited preferences profile. The items below are the meaningful remainder.

## Classification

| Classification | Remaining audit capabilities | Why they are in this category | Prerequisite to proceed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Feasible now, with source/content work** | Expand the tax knowledge catalogue, curated search, topic pages, general visual explanations, source-linked calculation explanations, multilingual content refinement, mobile/device testing, main-bundle optimisation, and low-data guided interviews. | These can remain educational and avoid personal or financial inputs. | Named content owner, source/version review process, test cases, and clear “reviewed, not live” labelling. |
| **Feasible after technical/product design** | Category calculator catalogue, multi-year rule presentation, difficult-scenario evaluation, and a tax knowledge graph. | They need a versioned authoritative knowledge model, source citations, change management, and evaluation coverage. | Curated source model, tax-year/version governance, withdrawal/change process, qualified content review, and regression test suite. |
| **Governance-gated** | Financial dashboard or profile expansion, document upload/extraction, field-mapped return preparation, wealth reconciliation, financial calculators using user values, notice classification/deadline workflow, response drafting, and personalised planning. | These would collect, infer from, or act on sensitive financial, document, notice, or taxpayer context. | A single-capability review with field inventory, lower-data alternative, qualified privacy/legal and security approvals, consent, retention/deletion, access control, threat model, human escalation, model evaluation, and launch controls. |
| **Deliberately out of scope for this app** | IRIS credential capture, OTP/password handling, operating IRIS, FBR filing/submission, asserting FBR acceptance, reproducing FBR validation, determining legal tax treatment/eligibility, calculating a binding personal deadline, predicting notice/outcome, or communicating with FBR for a visitor. | These contradict the product’s independent education-only role and create unacceptable authority, security, and legal-risk implications. | They should remain excluded unless the product is fundamentally re-scoped into an authorised, separately governed service with official integration and specialist accountability. |

## What is currently blocked versus merely unfinished

The **Pakistan tax knowledge base** is unfinished rather than blocked: it can grow safely as a reviewed, versioned set of sources and bounded explanations. The **tax knowledge graph** and **visual calculation explanations** are similarly feasible, but only once the source model is mature enough to avoid presenting stale or unsupported tax rules.

The audit’s **tax planning** capability splits into two paths. General, source-linked planning education is feasible and has begun through local planning reflections. Personal recommendations based on a visitor’s circumstances are governance-gated because they become personalised tax guidance. The same distinction applies to a generic calculator explanation versus a calculator that consumes a visitor’s financial values.

The following capability areas are currently blocked by the existing governance gate: return/document intelligence, wealth reconciliation, notice interpretation, personalised planning, and any profile expansion beyond the five approved preference fields. The profile itself is no longer blocked because its narrow design obtained approval and was implemented; extending it with identity, tax, financial, document, or filing fields would require a new separate approval.

## Practical next actions

| Path | Safe next implementation | Required owner decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content foundation | Add a reviewed, versioned topic catalogue for high-demand tax education with section-level citations and change notes. | Assign a content/source reviewer and review cadence. |
| Rule/explanation foundation | Build a source-versioned visual explanation and calculator-evaluation layer using no visitor values. | Approve a curated rule-source process and test corpus. |
| Sensitive capability | Select exactly one candidate—document review, reconciliation, or personalised planning—and run the full governance gate. | Obtain named privacy/legal, security, tax-content, and operations approvals. |

> **Important distinction:** “Unimplementable now” does not mean “impossible in software.” It means the feature cannot responsibly be added to this independent educational application until the required authority, data protections, source governance, and human accountability exist.

## Basis

This classification updates the project’s prior audit remainder assessment and applies the existing deferred-sensitive-capabilities governance gate. It also recognises the later implementation of the minimal, approved taxpayer-preferences profile, which did not authorise profile expansion or other sensitive capabilities.
