# Official Resource Hub: Source Review

**Reviewed:** 22 August 2026  
**Scope:** Company registration and income-tax-return preparation resources shown in Tax Return Saathi.  
**Editorial boundary:** This hub links users to official services and describes preparation steps at a high level. It does not collect personal data, submit applications, decide a tax position, or replace regulated professional advice.

| Topic | Resource selected for the app | Reason for inclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Company incorporation | [SECP: Registration of Company][1] | SECP’s official incorporation page explains name reservation, incorporation, eZfile resources, and links to official guides. |
| Name availability | [SECP eServices name search][2] | Official SECP online company-name search service. |
| Company-formation guides | [SECP guide books][3] | Official library for available company-formation material. |
| Taxpayer registration | [FBR: Register for Income Tax][4] | FBR lists pre-registration information and separately identifies requirements for companies and AOPs. |
| Return preparation and filing | [FBR: File Income Tax Return][5] | FBR’s filing-help page links to IRIS-login, completion, late-filing, record-keeping, and privacy topics. |
| Official filing portal | [FBR IRIS][6] | Official online portal used for the income-tax-return workflow. |

The displayed wording avoids promising an outcome or asserting that any linked process applies to every business. Users are told to confirm current requirements, fees, deadlines, and eligibility on the official portal before acting.
