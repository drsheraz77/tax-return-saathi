## Boundaries preserved

Each milestone retained the original authored calculation and review interface. The programme adds no tax amount, asset, liability, CNIC, NTN, bank, password, OTP, account, document, notice-text, upload, or IRIS credential collection. It does not file a return, operate IRIS, confirm portal acceptance, calculate a personal deadline, determine legal treatment, or provide tax/legal advice.

## Remaining work category

All remaining unimplemented partial-audit depth is either a future expansion of the safe knowledge/content foundation or a **governance-gated capability**. The latter includes taxpayer profiles, document intelligence, reconciliation, personalised planning, tax/notice determinations, and any feature that would store, process, infer from, or act on sensitive financial or identity information. The separately prepared governance requirements are the prerequisite for considering those features.
