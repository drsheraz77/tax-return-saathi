## Editorial decision

The app update will show a brief, dated **“Tax Year 2026 verified filing updates”** note. It will provide the official IRIS link, distinguish common individual/AOP and company dates, encourage accurate declarations, and advise users to check FBR’s latest notice for any lawful extension. No social-media comments, informal deadline claims, tax-rate changes, penalties, or unverified filing steps will be added.

## Integration verification

On 21 August 2026, the managed preview was opened after the update was added. The compact bilingual filing-update panel opened successfully and displayed the reviewed date, filing period, published due dates, official IRIS link, FBR source links, the official FBR public social-media announcement, and the selected newspaper report. The pre-existing tax assistant interface and its authored tax algorithm were not changed.
