# Privacy, feedback, and contact source review

## Official FBR taxpayer contact route

**Reviewed:** 24 August 2026

The public contact area may direct users seeking official tax or IRIS support to the FBR Contact Us page. The page lists the FBR Helpline as **051 111 772 772** nationally, **+92 51 111 772 772** internationally, and **helpline@fbr.gov.pk**, with stated hours of **8:00 AM–11:30 PM, Monday–Friday**. These details are presented as an external official support route, not as Tax Return Saathi support or a substitute for professional advice.

Source: <https://www.fbr.gov.pk/contact-us/142252/173964>
